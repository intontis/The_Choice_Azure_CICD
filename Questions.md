
## Question Tree and Prophecy Design — Version 1

### Concept

An individual, branching journey inspired by the red pill / blue pill concept.

Every visitor makes three decisions. Each decision determines the next question. The complete sequence of answers determines the final prophecy.

Community statistics are revealed only after the prophecy.

---

# 1. Starting Question

**A choice stands before you. One path reveals what is hidden; the other preserves the world you know.**

Which will you choose?

- RED: Look beyond the reality you know.
- BLUE: Preserve the reality you understand.

## RED branch — Question R1

**You have chosen to look closer. The first truth you uncover is incomplete. What do you do next?**

1. Follow the evidence, even if it unsettles you.
2. Seek another perspective before deciding what it means.

## BLUE branch — Question B1

**You have chosen the familiar world. But a small contradiction appears. How do you respond?**

1. Investigate the contradiction quietly.
2. Set it aside and protect the life you have built.

---

# 2. Third Questions

## Path RED → Option 1 — Question R11

**The evidence leads to a door no one else can see. What do you carry through it?**

1. Only what you have proven.
2. The people who trusted you.

## Path RED → Option 2 — Question R12

**Someone offers a convincing explanation, but it conflicts with what you witnessed.**

1. Challenge the explanation.
2. Keep listening until the pattern becomes clear.

## Path BLUE → Option 1 — Question B11

**Your investigation reveals that one familiar rule was never inevitable.**

1. Test a different way.
2. Wait until you understand the consequences.

## Path BLUE → Option 2 — Question B12

**The contradiction fades, but the question remains.**

1. Return to it when the moment is right.
2. Let it go and choose peace.

---

# 3. Complete Journey Paths

Each journey consists of three decisions.

The first character represents the initial RED or BLUE choice.
The second and third characters represent option 1 or option 2.

| Path | Decisions |
|---|---|
| R11 | RED → Option 1 → Option 1 |
| R12 | RED → Option 1 → Option 2 |
| R21 | RED → Option 2 → Option 1 |
| R22 | RED → Option 2 → Option 2 |
| B11 | BLUE → Option 1 → Option 1 |
| B12 | BLUE → Option 1 → Option 2 |
| B21 | BLUE → Option 2 → Option 1 |
| B22 | BLUE → Option 2 → Option 2 |

---

# 4. The Prophet — Initial Prophecies

Each completed path maps to one unique prophecy.

### R11 — The Cartographer

You move towards the unknown, relying on evidence rather than promises. You are willing to enter unfamiliar territory, but remember that even the best map cannot predict every turn.

### R12 — The Keeper of Names

You crossed the threshold, but you did not leave everyone behind. You carry the people and experiences that shaped your journey. Remember that responsibility can be both an anchor and a compass.

### R21 — The Skeptic's Flame

You question explanations that others accept without hesitation. Your curiosity keeps you moving, but remember that doubt is a tool for discovery, not a destination.

### R22 — The Listener at the Gate

You seek understanding before making your final decision. You know that patience can reveal patterns that haste would destroy. Just make sure you recognize when the moment to act has arrived.

### B11 — The Quiet Disruptor

You appear to preserve the familiar, yet quietly investigate what others overlook. You may discover that the greatest changes do not always begin with rebellion. Sometimes they begin with one unanswered question.

### B12 — The Steward of Consequences

You recognize that truth and responsibility must coexist. You are willing to question what you know, but you understand that every decision has consequences beyond yourself.

### B21 — The Returner

You chose stability, but something kept calling you back. Not every question must be answered immediately. Some truths wait until you are ready to confront them.

### B22 — The Keeper of Still Waters

You value peace and the world you have built. There is strength in knowing what you wish to preserve. But remember: comfort and certainty are not always the same thing.

---

# 5. Community Statistics

Statistics are revealed only after the visitor receives their prophecy.

The results page will initially display:

- Percentage of visitors who chose RED versus BLUE.
- Distribution of answers to each question.
- Most common complete journey.
- Distribution of the eight prophecies.

Only completed journeys contribute to the statistics.

---

# 6. Design Rules

1. Every visitor follows an individual journey.
2. Every question has two possible answers.
3. Every answer leads to another question or completes the journey.
4. Every valid completed path maps to exactly one prophecy.
5. The same complete path produces the same prophecy.
6. A visitor cannot submit two answers to the same question within one journey.
7. Community statistics remain hidden until the prophecy is displayed.
